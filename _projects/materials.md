At ICFO, during a summer fellowship in Frank Koppens’ group (July–September 2021), I worked on CVD graphene growth and transfer to silicon/silicon-dioxide substrates, nanofabrication, and Python data analysis.

My bachelor’s thesis at the Universidad de Salamanca (2020–2021), _TMD-based optoelectronic devices_, investigated faster characterization of monolayers by comparing optical microscopy and Raman spectra. I also gained experience with exfoliation, cleanroom processing, and Python image analysis.

At CIC nanoGUNE (July–August 2020), I characterized electrical and Raman properties of MoS₂ and graphite with intercalated organic molecules, using viscoelastic stamping and electrical measurements in a vacuum probe station.
