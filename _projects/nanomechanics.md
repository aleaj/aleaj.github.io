For my master’s thesis in Adrian Bachtold’s Quantum Nanomechanics group at ICFO (January–September 2022), I worked on fabrication toward a mechanical qubit based on a double quantum dot embedded in a carbon nanotube.

The project explored integration of a superconducting circuit to drive and read out the mechanical resonator. I designed and characterized a high-inductance superconducting resonator using electromagnetic simulation and radio-frequency measurements in a helium dewar.
