My current research in Johannes Fink’s group at ISTA focuses on superconducting circuits, quantum optics, and remote qubit entanglement.

During my 2022–2023 internship in the group, I worked on entangling two superconducting qubits using a two-mode squeezed source in a continuous waveguide QED architecture. My work included circuit design, fabrication, characterization, and measurement.

Our work is published in [Physical Review X](https://doi.org/10.1103/r4jt-j39w), with the preprint available as [arXiv:2510.07139](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07139).
